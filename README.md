# HR Analytics Dashboard | Employee Attrition & Performance

## 🔍 Project Overview
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built to analyze employee data with a focus on attrition, performance, and demographics.  
The dashboard enables HR teams to identify workforce trends, reduce attrition risks, and support data-driven decisions.

## 📊 Key Highlights
- Overall employee statistics and attrition rate  
- Attrition analysis by department, job role, tenure, and travel frequency  
- Performance and satisfaction tracking over multiple years  
- Demographic insights including age and marital status  

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI  
- Excel / CSV  

## 💡 Business Insights
- Certain departments and job roles show higher attrition rates, indicating potential workload or satisfaction issues.  
- Employees with lower job and environment satisfaction are more likely to leave the organization.  
- Attrition tends to be higher during early tenure years, highlighting the importance of onboarding and early engagement.  
- Performance ratings show a noticeable relationship with manager feedback and job satisfaction trends.  

## 🎯 Use Case
- HR decision-making and workforce planning  
- Employee retention strategies  
- Data analytics portfolio project
